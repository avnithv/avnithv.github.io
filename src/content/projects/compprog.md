I reached Master on Codeforces and Platinum on USACO in fall 2023, and began representing my school at onsite competitions around the same time. More recently I've focused on AtCoder, which fits a busier schedule with more frequent contests and a math and observation focused style I prefer over Codeforces.

I've also given back to my community. I've written problems for public contests including TJIOI and BioCode, run by the TJHSST Computer Team and Bioinformatics clubs I captained from 2023 to 2025, and went from USACO participant to a coach on its problemsetting team - proposing problems and preparing and testing others' for the monthly contests taken by thousands of students. I also teach as an instructor at AlphaStar Academy, where I've built curriculum and recorded USACO solution videos.
